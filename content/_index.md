<p>A wireless printer that shows offline is often a queue or port problem, not a dead printer. I verify the printer, the PC, and the job path in that order.</p>
<h2>What is usually failing</h2>
<ul><li>Printer shows offline although Wi-Fi is connected</li>
<li>Print queue is stuck or jobs disappear</li>
<li>Works from one PC but not another Windows PC</li></ul>
<h2>What you get</h2>
<ul><li>One Windows PC and one printer tested end to end</li>
<li>Queue, port, driver, and power/sleep checks</li>
<li>A printed test page and a short recovery sheet</li></ul>
<h2>Scope and boundaries</h2>
<p>This is remote help for one Windows PC and one printer. I do not change your router unless the chosen fix requires it and you approve it. I do not ask for account passwords; use a temporary session or attend the session.</p>
<h2>How the session works</h2>
<p>You send the device, OS, exact symptom, and what you already tried. We confirm the scope and a fixed quote before booking. The session is screen-shared and recorded only with your consent. You receive a short handover note with the tested result and rollback steps.</p>
